Standardised form under other rules

Concept IRI: https://openric.org/vocab/name-type/standardised
In scheme: OpenRiC Name-Type Vocabulary (https://openric.org/vocab/name-type/)

The name as it is constructed under rules other than those used for the authorised form, such as a library's.

Source elements: ISAAR(CPF) 5.1.4.
