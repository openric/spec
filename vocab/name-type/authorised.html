Authorised form

Concept IRI: https://openric.org/vocab/name-type/authorised
In scheme: OpenRiC Name-Type Vocabulary (https://openric.org/vocab/name-type/)

The standard form of name that identifies the entity, built by the rules the describing institution follows.
