Description

Concept IRI: https://openric.org/vocab/date-type/descriptive
In scheme: OpenRiC Date-Type Vocabulary (https://openric.org/vocab/date-type/)

The dates at which the description itself was prepared or revised, not the dates of the records it describes.

Source element: ISAD(G) 3.7.3 Date(s) of descriptions.

Machine-readable: date-type.ttl (SKOS, Turtle).
