Accumulation

Concept IRI: https://openric.org/vocab/date-type/accumulation
In scheme: OpenRiC Date-Type Vocabulary (https://openric.org/vocab/date-type/)

The dates at which the records were accumulated, as distinct from when they were created.

Source element: ISAD(G) 3.1.3 Date(s), where the dates are of accumulation.
