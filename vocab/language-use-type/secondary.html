Secondary language

Concept IRI: https://openric.org/vocab/language-use-type/secondary
In scheme: OpenRiC Language-Use-Type Vocabulary (https://openric.org/vocab/language-use-type/)

A language used alongside the primary one without being separable into its own part, for example for loanwords or a localised passage.

Source: Sylvain Loiseau, RiC-O issue #146 (November 2025).
