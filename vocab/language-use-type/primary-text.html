Primary text

Concept IRI: https://openric.org/vocab/language-use-type/primary-text
In scheme: OpenRiC Language-Use-Type Vocabulary (https://openric.org/vocab/language-use-type/)

The main language of the text or of the activity: the language in which the record, or the performance, chiefly is.
