Translator

Concept IRI: https://openric.org/vocab/role-type/translator
In scheme: OpenRiC Role-Type Vocabulary (https://openric.org/vocab/role-type/)

An agent who rendered a record, or a performance, into another language.

Pairs with openricx:relationHasLanguage on the same relation, which names the language.

Machine-readable: role-type.ttl (SKOS, Turtle).
