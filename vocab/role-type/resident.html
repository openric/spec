Resident

Concept IRI: https://openric.org/vocab/role-type/resident
In scheme: OpenRiC Role-Type Vocabulary (https://openric.org/vocab/role-type/)

A person living in an institution or place of care during an episode of care.

Care in an institution is modelled as an activity whose participants carry their roles, not as a kinship property; see the OpenRiC extension ontology, section 2.15.

Machine-readable: role-type.ttl (SKOS, Turtle).
