Donor

Concept IRI: https://openric.org/vocab/role-type/donor
In scheme: OpenRiC Role-Type Vocabulary (https://openric.org/vocab/role-type/)

An agent who gave records or other material to the holding institution.

The role on the relation between an acquisition or custody-transfer activity and the agent who gave the material.
