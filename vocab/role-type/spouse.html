Spouse

Concept IRI: https://openric.org/vocab/role-type/spouse
In scheme: OpenRiC Role-Type Vocabulary (https://openric.org/vocab/role-type/)

A party to a marriage or civil union, in the event that creates it.

Use with openricx:relationHasAgentRole on the relation between the marriage event and each party. Use bride or groom where the record names the party that way.

Machine-readable: role-type.ttl (SKOS, Turtle).
