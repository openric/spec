Witness

Concept IRI: https://openric.org/vocab/role-type/witness
In scheme: OpenRiC Role-Type Vocabulary (https://openric.org/vocab/role-type/)

An agent who attests to an event or to the making of a record, such as a marriage, a signature or a court hearing.

The role on the relation between the event, or the record's creation, and the agent.
