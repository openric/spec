Performer

Concept IRI: https://openric.org/vocab/role-type/performer
In scheme: OpenRiC Role-Type Vocabulary (https://openric.org/vocab/role-type/)

An agent who took part in a performance, such as a recital, a play or a recorded broadcast.

The role on a rico:PerformanceRelation, or on another relation between a performance activity and the agent.
