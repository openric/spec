Editor

Concept IRI: https://openric.org/vocab/role-type/editor
In scheme: OpenRiC Role-Type Vocabulary (https://openric.org/vocab/role-type/)

An agent who prepared a record or a publication for issue, compiling or revising the work of others.

As a creation role, the value of rico:creationWithRole; on other relations, of openricx:relationHasAgentRole.

Machine-readable: role-type.ttl (SKOS, Turtle).
